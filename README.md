# SEZA.SYSTEM

**SEZA.SYSTEM** is an original creative universe and visual archive built around **Aureya**, the **Celestial White Dragon**, **Genesis**, and the **FoldCell** visual language.

This repository documents the development, artwork, worldbuilding, visual concepts, and creative process behind the project.

## Core Universe

### AUREYA

Aureya is the central presence of the SEZA.SYSTEM universe.

### Celestial White Dragon

The Celestial White Dragon is a central figure within Aureya's visual mythology and Genesis storyline.

### GENESIS

Genesis is the origin sequence of the universe — the beginning of Aureya and the primordial dragons.

### FoldCell

FoldCell is the visual language of the world, based on geometric and origami-inspired structures, folding patterns, tessellation, modular geometry, and fractal forms.

FoldCell is not simply a style applied to individual subjects. It defines the visual geometry of the environment, creatures, structures, and world itself.
