## VISUAL CONSISTENCY

These three reference artworks establish the primary visual direction of SEZA.SYSTEM.

Future works may vary in composition, environment, lighting, story, and scene design while maintaining the core identity defined by the **SEZA.SYSTEM Master Blueprint**.

The visual identity is based on:

**Aureya + Celestial White Dragon + Genesis + celestial color system + ancient mountain world + flowing energy + FoldCell geometry.**

## FOLDCELL

FoldCell is the geometric visual language of SEZA.SYSTEM.

Its principles include:

* Origami-inspired geometry
* Folded surfaces
* Modular structures
* Tessellation
* Polyhedral forms
* Crystalline geometry
* Fractal patterns
* Repeating geometric structures

FoldCell applies to the world itself rather than functioning only as a decorative style.
